The Finlay Series: Natural Feldspar Aesthetics
Available in four signature tones
 
 
 
 
Visual Aesthetic and Pattern
The Finlay design masterfully captures the organic, earthy essence of natural feldspar. Set against a luminous, soft off-white base, the surface is densely scattered with angular, crystalline-like aggregates. These fragmented "chips" vary in size from fine granules to prominent, chunky inclusions, mirroring the authentic cleavage and mineral composition of raw feldspar stones. The color palette of the aggregate relies on subtle, harmonious neutral tones—ranging from pale ash greys to warm, sandy beiges and muted taupe —creating a sophisticated, terrestrial look that is both dynamic and visually grounding.
 
 
Surface and Tactility
What truly elevates this design is the advanced 3D textured surface. Rather than a flat, digital replication, the 3D finish provides a tactile dimension that aligns perfectly with the visual depth of the feldspar inclusions. This texture mimics the natural clefts, granular feel, and subtle undulations of authentic stone, enhancing the realism of the tile and interacting beautifully with ambient light.
Format and Application
Presented in an expansive 800x1600mm format, this slab is engineered for seamless continuity. The large-format scale significantly reduces grout lines, allowing the intricate, terrazzo-like feldspar pattern to flow uninterrupted across large floors or grand feature walls.
